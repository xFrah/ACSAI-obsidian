Stiffness vs compliance
stiffness is newton per millimiters. basically it measures how much force it takes to move?
Compliance is the opposite, it measures how it reacts and complies to force applied on it.

You can also program these to be directional.

Basically you can make trajectories using this, and make the robot learn how it should move based on hand-made trajectories with compliant joints/transmissions.

encoders are circuits that report on the angular positions of the joints.

For example our hands are compliant, since if we close our hand, our hand stops and kinda adapts to the object.
